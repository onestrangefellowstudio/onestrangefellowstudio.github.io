Eggsplosion Dino Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Eggsplosion Dino is a arcade game made by One Strange Fellow (also written OneStrangeFellow), an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels reached, scores, coins and the dinos you have hatched) is saved only on your own phone. It is never sent to us, and removing the app removes it.

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and the game has no accounts.

Ads

Eggsplosion Dino is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob: optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward, short full-screen ads between levels, and banner ads outside the gameplay screen. To show and count these ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID and other device information, your approximate location (based on your internet address), how you interact with ads, and crash and performance information.

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads at any time in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Analytics

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, we use Google Analytics for Firebase. It records anonymous events such as levels started and completed, hints used and ads watched, together with device information and the advertising ID. We use this only in summary form and cannot identify you from it.

Measuring our own adverts

If you install Eggsplosion Dino after seeing one of our adverts on Facebook or Instagram, the Meta SDK in the game lets Meta tell us that the advert led to an install and to a few in-game milestones, such as finishing the tutorial or reaching certain levels. This uses your device's advertising ID and basic device information, and helps us know which adverts work.

Who else handles this information

  • Google (AdMob and Firebase): Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Meta Platforms: Meta Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information.

Children

Eggsplosion Dino is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and want information removed, contact us.

Your choices and rights

You can limit ad tracking and reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings, change your ad consent choice inside the game where offered, or stop all collection by uninstalling the game. If you live in the UK or EU you have rights over your personal data, including access and deletion; contact us and we will help, including passing your request to Google or Meta where they hold the data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
